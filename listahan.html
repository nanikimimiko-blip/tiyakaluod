ABOUT
Arthur Nery
Singer | Songwriter

Arthur Nery or Arthur Madrigalejos Nery is a defining voice of modern OPM, known for his smooth vocals, emotive songwriting, and genre-blending sound. Rising to mainstream success with hits like “Higa” and “Binhi,” he reached record-breaking heights with “Pagsamo,” the first OPM song to surpass 500,000 daily Spotify streams for four consecutive days and over 300 million total streams. His impact extends beyond streaming, with sold-out concerts at New Frontier Theater (2022) and Smart Araneta Coliseum (2024). In 2024, Arthur released his acclaimed sophomore album II: The Second, showcasing his grow th as an artist and solidifying his place among OPM’s new-generation icons.



Birth Date: January 28, 1997
